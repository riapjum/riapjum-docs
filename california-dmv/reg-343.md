\# How to Complete California REG 343



\## What is Form REG 343?

California Form REG 343 is the \*\*Application for Title or Registration\*\*.  

It is required whenever a vehicle is:



\- sold or transferred  

\- brought into California from another state  

\- newly purchased from a private party  

\- being registered after a long lapse  

\- assigned to a new owner due to inheritance or gift  



This is the core form the California DMV uses to establish ownership and registration.



\## Information you will need

Before completing REG 343, gather:



\- \*\*Vehicle details:\*\* VIN, year, make, model, body type, odometer reading  

\- \*\*Owner details:\*\* full legal name, address, driver license/ID number  

\- \*\*Purchase details:\*\* purchase price, date of sale, seller information  

\- \*\*Smog information:\*\* smog certificate (if required)  

\- \*\*Out‑of‑state documents:\*\* title, registration card, or transfer paperwork  

\- \*\*Lienholder information:\*\* if the vehicle is financed  



\## Registered owner information

The registered owner must provide:



\- Full legal name  

\- Physical California address  

\- Driver license or ID number  

\- Date of purchase or transfer  

\- Signature (ink required)  



If there are multiple owners, all must be listed and sign according to the ownership type (AND/OR).



\## Legal owner (Lienholder)

If the vehicle is financed, the legal owner (lienholder) section must include:



\- Bank or finance company name  

\- Address  

\- Electronic Lien Title (ELT) number if applicable  



If the vehicle is paid off, this section is left blank.



\## Vehicle information

This section identifies the vehicle:



\- VIN  

\- Year  

\- Make  

\- Model  

\- Body type model (e.g., 4D, PK, VN)  

\- Odometer reading  

\- Fuel type  

\- Weight (for commercial vehicles)  



The DMV uses this to verify identity, calculate fees, and determine smog requirements.



\## Use tax and fees

California collects \*\*use tax\*\* on private‑party purchases.  

You must provide:



\- Purchase price  

\- County of residence  

\- Whether the transaction qualifies for exemption (gift, family transfer, inheritance, etc.)



The DMV uses this information to calculate:



\- Use tax  

\- Registration fees  

\- Title fees  

\- Transfer fees  



\## Out‑of‑state vehicles

If the vehicle is coming from another state, you must provide:



\- Out‑of‑state title  

\- Out‑of‑state registration card  

\- VIN verification (REG 31 or CHP inspection)  

\- Smog certificate (if required)  



REG 343 is the main form used to bring the vehicle into California’s system.



\## Certification and signature

The registered owner must sign and date the form, certifying that all information is true.  

Ink signatures are required — DMV does not accept digital signatures for REG 343.
