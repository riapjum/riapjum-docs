# How to Complete Texas Form VTR‑346

## What is Form VTR‑346?
Texas Form VTR‑346 is the **Statement of Fact for Out‑of‑State Registration**.  
It is used when a vehicle is **registered in another state** but needs to be titled in Texas. This form explains why the vehicle does not have Texas registration and confirms the details needed for the county tax office to process the title transfer.

You typically use VTR‑346 when:
- The vehicle is coming from out of state  
- The buyer recently moved to Texas  
- The vehicle was purchased in another state and brought into Texas  
- The vehicle has valid out‑of‑state plates but no Texas registration  

## Information you will need
To complete VTR‑346, gather:

- **Vehicle details:** VIN, year, make, model  
- **Out‑of‑state registration details:** state, plate number, expiration date  
- **Owner information:** full legal name, address, and driver license/ID number  
- **Reason for out‑of‑state registration:** purchase, relocation, military, temporary residence, etc.  
- **Supporting documents:** out‑of‑state title or registration receipt  

## Owner information
The owner must provide:

- Full legal name  
- Texas or out‑of‑state address  
- Driver license or government ID number  
- Signature and date (ink signature required)  

This confirms who is submitting the statement and ties the vehicle to the correct owner.

## Vehicle information
This section identifies the vehicle:

- VIN  
- Year  
- Make  
- Model  
- Current out‑of‑state plate number  
- State of registration  
- Registration expiration date  

The county uses this information to verify the vehicle’s status and determine tax and title requirements.

## Reason for out‑of‑state registration
You must select or describe the reason the vehicle is registered outside Texas. Common reasons include:

- Recently purchased out of state  
- Owner recently moved to Texas  
- Vehicle was previously titled in another state  
- Military personnel stationed in Texas  
- Temporary residence in another state  

This helps the county determine whether additional documents or taxes are required.

## Certification and signature
The owner must sign and date the form, certifying that all information is true.  
Ink signature is required — TXDMV does not accept digital signatures for this form.
