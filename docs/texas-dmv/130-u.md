\# How to Complete Texas Form 130-U



\## What is Form 130-U?

Texas Form 130-U is the official \*\*Application for Texas Title and/or Registration\*\*. It’s required any time a vehicle changes ownership — private sale, gift, inheritance, or even certain dealer transactions. The buyer submits this form to the county tax office to transfer the title and register the vehicle.



\## Information you will need

Before starting, gather the following:



\- \*\*Vehicle details:\*\* VIN, year, make, model, body style, and current odometer reading  

\- \*\*Sales details:\*\* sales price, trade-in information (if any), and date of sale  

\- \*\*Tax details:\*\* whether the transaction qualifies for gift exemption, even trade, or standard motor vehicle sales tax  

\- \*\*County information:\*\* the county where the buyer resides  

\- \*\*Supporting documents:\*\* title, lien release (if applicable), and ID for both buyer and seller



\## Seller information

The seller must provide:



\- Full legal name  

\- Address  

\- Signature and date (ink signature strongly preferred by TXDMV)  

\- Any lien release documents if the vehicle had a lien  

\- Accurate odometer disclosure if required



\## Buyer information

The buyer must provide:



\- Full legal name  

\- Address  

\- Driver license or government ID number  

\- Signature and date  

\- County of residence  

\- Whether they want to register the vehicle immediately or only transfer the title



\## Vehicle information

This section identifies the vehicle and is used for tax calculation:



\- VIN  

\- Year, make, model, body style  

\- Odometer reading at the time of sale  

\- Whether the vehicle is new, used, rebuilt, or has prior damage  

\- Any existing liens or financing information  

\- Sales price and tax category (standard tax, gift, even trade, etc.)
